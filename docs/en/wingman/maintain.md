## Maintain steps

Keep the application image and certificate inputs current through the [infrastructure runbook](.././validation/#connect-the-existing-owner). Review and hash a fresh saved plan, apply it through the existing owner, then repeat [verification](.././verify/). Preserve old material privately for rollback.
